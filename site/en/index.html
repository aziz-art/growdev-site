Frequently asked questions

Answers before your first question.

Short answers to the most common questions about Odoo, websites and mobile apps.

What is Odoo and what does it do for my business?

Odoo is a modular business management software (ERP). It brings sales, purchasing, stock, invoicing, manufacturing, human resources and your website into one tool. You only switch on the modules you need, and they share the same data: no more double entry, no more scattered files.

Is Odoo a good fit for a Tunisian small or medium business?

Yes, as soon as your sales, stock or invoicing are spread across separate files and tools. Odoo is rolled out step by step, handles several currencies and taxes, and its interface is available in Arabic, French and English. We start by analysing your business to confirm it is the right choice.

How much does an Odoo project, a website or an app cost?

The price depends on the scope: number of modules, volume of data to migrate, custom development and training. We do not publish a fixed rate. After a first conversation, we send a clear quote split into phases: in Tunisian dinars (DT) for Tunisia, in euros or dollars internationally.

How long does it take to set up Odoo?

It depends on the number of modules. As a rough guide, a simple scope (for example CRM, sales and invoicing) can be ready in a few weeks, while a full project with data migration and custom development takes several months. We deliver in short steps so you can use the first modules quickly.

Do you work with companies outside Tunisia?

Yes. We support Tunisian businesses first, then companies across Africa and the rest of the world, remotely. We work in French, Arabic and English, and run meetings by video call to suit your time zone.

Can my website or app be in Arabic, French and English?

Yes. We build multilingual websites, shops and apps, with right-to-left layout for Arabic, several currencies and content that is easy to translate. Odoo, WordPress and PrestaShop all handle several languages, with dedicated extensions when needed.

WordPress, PrestaShop or a custom app: which one should I choose?

WordPress suits showcase sites and blogs that you edit yourself. PrestaShop is built for selling online, with catalogue, cart and payment. A custom web or mobile app makes sense when your processes are specific and existing tools fall short. We advise you once we understand your need.

What happens after the launch?

We train your team, set up backups and security, then keep following up: fixes, updates and new features, at the pace of your business. You keep the same point of contact from start to finish.
