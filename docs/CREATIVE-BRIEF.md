## 1. Project overview

**Purpose.** Turn a visitor, first an owner or manager of a Tunisian small or medium business, into a project conversation by e-mail. GrowDev has no website today; this one has to create trust, explain seven services without jargon, and be found in search and in AI assistants.

**Campaign context**

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Always-on website (not a time-boxed campaign) |
| Funnel | Awareness (hero, services) → consideration (method, "compose your system", simulator, FAQ) → conversion (project brief by e-mail) |
| Platforms | Web, mobile first. Link previews on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Facebook, X |
| Address | `growdev.werchti.tn` (GitHub Pages, DNS at OVH) |
| Flight dates | Live as soon as the DNS record is added. No end date |
| Budget | No hosting cost; the domain is already owned |

**Success looks like.** A visitor can say in one sentence what GrowDev does, finds the service that matches their problem in under 30 seconds, and sends a brief without having to know any IT vocabulary.

## 2. Target audience

| Priority | Who | Language |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Tunisia** | Owner, manager or "the person in charge of IT" in an SME of 5–50 people: trading and distribution, retail, light manufacturing, services, clinics | French and Arabic, some English |
| **2. Africa** | The same profile in the Maghreb and in French-speaking Africa; Arabic-speaking markets | French, Arabic |
| **3. World** | Start-ups and agencies abroad that want Odoo, web or mobile work from a Tunisian team | English, French |

**Their situation**

- *Problem:* sales, stock and invoices live in separate Excel files and WhatsApp chats; double entry; late invoices; no online presence, or a site nobody maintains.
- *Desire:* see the whole business in one place, sell online, have an app, stop depending on one person's spreadsheet.
- *Current behaviour:* ask friends and Facebook groups, call a local agency, hire a freelancer on a marketplace.
- *Barriers:* "how much will it cost?", "will it take a year?", "I won't understand it", no references to check, a project that fails halfway.

**What resonates:** plain words, a concrete first step, prices talked about in DT, an Arabic version that reads like it was written in Arabic, and seeing what they would get before they write.

**Audience insight:** *"I don't know exactly what I need. I only know my files are a mess, and I don't want to be sold software I don't understand."*

## 3. Messaging

**Key message (one sentence):** GrowDev builds the digital tools your business needs (Odoo, websites, shops and apps), in your language, step by step.

| Priority | Element | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | « Votre croissance, notre code. » · «نموّكم، برمجتنا.» · "Your growth, our code." |
| 2 | Value proposition | We digitalise your business with Odoo ERP, websites, online shops, mobile apps and automation, from the first conversation to going live. |
| 3 | Benefit 1 | One tool for sales, purchasing, stock and invoicing (Odoo), instead of scattered files. |
| 4 | Benefit 2 | Everything under one roof: Odoo, custom web, WordPress, PrestaShop, mobile, automation. |
| 5 | Benefit 3 | Delivered in phases, in French, Arabic or English, quoted in DT in Tunisia. |
| 6 | Proof | A visible five-step method; a live tool that draws *your* system; a simulator that shows hours and money saved from your own numbers; no cookies, no trackers. Client case studies are **pending** (see §8). |
| 7 | CTA | « Parlons de votre projet » · «ناقش مشروعك معنا» · "Talk about my project" → composes an e-mail brief |

**Claims we can make**

- GrowDev is a software company based in Tunisia.
- Services: Odoo ERP, digital transformation, web development, WordPress, PrestaShop, mobile apps, automation.
- Work in French, Arabic and English; Tunisia first, then Africa and abroad, remotely.
- Quotes in DT for Tunisia, in € or $ abroad; no fixed price list, each project is quoted after a first conversation.
- No cookies and no trackers on this site.

**Claims to avoid** (until they are true and provable)

- "Official Odoo partner", "certified", "#1", "leader".
- Number of clients or projects, client logos, testimonials, ratings.
- "From X DT", guaranteed deadlines, guaranteed savings. The simulator is an estimate built from the visitor's own inputs, and says so.

**Tone of voice.** Warm, direct, calm: a competent colleague who explains things simply. No buzzwords, no hype, no fear. French: *vous*, short sentences. English: plain, international. Arabic: simple Modern Standard Arabic, written natively (not translated word for word), addressing the reader in the plural.

## 4. Creative direction

**Format.** Responsive single page, three language versions. Breakpoints checked at 390, 768 and 1440 px. Link-preview image 1200×630 JPEG, one per language.

**Visual style**

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Polish | Highly produced, but not glossy |
| Mood | Confident, optimistic, calm |
| Energy | Medium: motion has a job (explain, guide), and stops when asked (`prefers-reduced-motion`) |
| Density | Minimal to medium: one idea per screen |
| Dark ↔ bright | Dark canvas, bright growth accents |
| Corporate ↔ casual | Friendly-professional |

**Signature idea: growth as architecture.** The bars and arrow of the logo become the page's structure. Hero bars are the seven services, each a link. The "compose your system" map grows as modules are chosen. The method timeline draws itself with the scroll. Coins stack in the simulator.

**Palette** (taken from the logo): night blue `#020b18` / `#041424` / `#06192c`; growth green `#2fe56a` → `#10ce97` → sky `#00bafc` (the gradient); gold `#f2b230` for coins and "parallel" work; text `#f2f6fa`, `#b9c6d3`, `#8fa2b5`.

**Type.** Sora (headlines), IBM Plex Sans (text), IBM Plex Mono (labels and numbers), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Arabic only). Self-hosted, no external font service.

**Shapes.** Asymmetric corners (`6px 28px`), echoing the leaf-and-arrow gesture of the emblem. No uniform "pill and card" grid.

**Reference direction**

✅ *Like this:* one idea per screen; the product shown as a live diagram instead of a screenshot; large numerals; one accent family on a calm dark background; text that a non-technical owner can read aloud.

❌ *Not like this:* stock photos of handshakes and laptops; purple-to-blue gradients with floating blobs; rows of identical icon cards; fake client logos or reviews; neon "hacker" looks; centred everything; a cookie banner.

**Visual must-haves**

- [x] Logo emblem in the header and share images, always on the dark background
- [x] A hero that shows growth within three seconds (bars + arrow + coin)
- [x] Each service reachable in one click and linked to the composer
- [x] A visible contact path in every section's reach (header button, hero, composer, footer)
- [x] Arabic page truly right-to-left (layout, arrows, timeline, chart labels), not a mirrored afterthought

## 6. Requirements checklist

**Must-haves**

- [x] Three complete languages (French, Arabic, English), each at its own URL
- [x] DT is the first and default currency; MAD, DZD, EGP, XOF, EUR and USD available
- [x] Works without cookies, analytics, or third-party requests
- [x] Readable and usable without a mouse, and with animations switched off
- [x] Fast on mid-range phones and slow connections
- [x] Findable: SEO, GEO and AEO layer (see `SEO-GEO-AEO.md`)

**Nice-to-haves**

- [ ] WhatsApp contact button (needs the number; very common for Tunisian SMEs)
- [ ] Case studies with client permission
- [ ] A short video demo of the composer

**Things to avoid:** invented proof, a pricing table, jargon in headlines, auto-playing sound, any pop-up.

**Brand guidelines.** Emblem circle on the dark background only; never recoloured or stretched. Wordmark "Grow" in white and "Dev" in green. Colours and type as in §4.

**Compliance.** The site sets no cookies and collects nothing. The only storage is the chosen language (`localStorage`, key `gd-lang`). The project brief is composed in the visitor's own mail client and only leaves their device when they press send. If forms, analytics or newsletters are added later, review Tunisian personal-data rules (organic law 2004-63) and get legal advice first.

## 7. Success criteria

**Primary KPI:** qualified enquiries, meaning e-mails that arrive with a project brief.

| Metric | Proposed target (adjust to your goals) | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified enquiries per month | 3+ within 90 days of launch | none (new site) |
| Search Console impressions, by language | growing every month; all three languages present | none |
| Share of visitors on mobile whose page loads under 2.5 s (LCP) | 75%+ (Google's "good" threshold) | to be measured |
| Accessibility | no serious or critical issues in an automated scan | measured before launch |

**How we evaluate.** Count the briefs received (each has a recognisable subject line), read Search Console monthly, and run the checker (`npm run check`) before every change. Because the site has no analytics on purpose, the e-mails themselves are the conversion data.
